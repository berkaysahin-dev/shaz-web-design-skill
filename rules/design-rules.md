# Shaz Agency Web Design — Mandatory Design Rules & Anti-Pattern Matrix

This document defines the strict, non-negotiable design laws governing all user interfaces, components, and code output generated by this skill. Every rule has an explicit anti-pattern definition and its authorized replacement pattern.

---

## 1. Zero Fluff Text (High-Density Editorial Copy)

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- Multi-sentence explanatory paragraphs describing what the product does in generic marketing terms (e.g., *"We leverage cutting-edge paradigms to empower next-generation stakeholders with seamless synergy..."*).
- Repetitive feature descriptions where the title and description say the exact same thing.
- Generic placeholders like *"Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet"* or *"Your journey starts here"*.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Title-Dominant Layout:** The headline carries 80% of the message. Make it sharp, direct, and authoritative (e.g., *"Distributed Consensus at Sub-Millisecond Latency"*).
- **Single-Sentence Telemetry:** If body text is required, limit it to a single sentence that conveys a technical fact, concrete outcome, or architectural metric.
- **Data Over Adjectives:** Replace descriptive words with concrete parameters (e.g., instead of *"Extremely fast engine"*, use *"140µs P99 Execution"*).

---

## 2. Zero Badges, Pills, Eyebrows, or Fake Coordinates

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- Small floating pill containers placed above headings (e.g., `<span class="rounded-full bg-blue-100 text-blue-700 px-3 py-1">✨ New Release v2.0</span>`).
- **Eyebrow / Kicker Sub-Labels Above Headings:** Small monospaced tags or category text directly above titles (e.g., `ATELIER DOCTRINE · SECTION 03`, `OBSERVATORY SPECIFICATION`, `SECTION 02`). Headings must stand clean, authoritative, and direct without decorative micro-labels.
- **Fake Telemetry & Coordinates:** Decorative GPS coordinates (e.g., `46°12'09"N · 6°08'44"E`), synthetic serial numbers, machine IDs, or fake sensor logs used as filler text.
- Status chips or pill tags placed inside navigation bars or headers.
- "Best Choice", "Most Popular", "Recommended" decorative badges stuck onto pricing cards.
- Colored circular status indicators (green/amber/red dots) placed next to labels purely for decoration.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Clean Headline Primacy:** Headings lead directly without kicker prefixes. Hierarchy is achieved solely through typographic scale, optical weight, and editorial white space.
- **Surface Elevation:** In pricing or feature comparisons, emphasize the flagship tier by inverting its container surface (e.g., dark obsidian core amidst light stone trays) or through subtle 1px border contrast, never via a pill badge or kicker tag.
- **Structural Partitioning:** Use clean architectural layout grids and negative space to organize content, without artificial section numbers.

---

## 3. Color Architecture: Category-Driven Palette with Strict Neon Prohibition

### Core Principle:
There is no arbitrary restriction to a fixed set of colors. Any color family may be used, provided that it is **strictly derived from the project's sector, product materiality, and design context**.
- **Haute Horlogerie / Luxury Watches:** Deep obsidian (`#070709`), brushed titanium (`#A1A1AA`), warm champagne/matte gold (`#C5A880`), smoked sapphire.
- **Architectural / Industrial Studio:** Raw concrete gray, basalt black, textured chalk, natural paper beiges.
- **Biotech / Scientific / Medical:** Deep petroleum blue, surgical white, calm sage, deep navy.
- **Automotive / Performance:** Matte graphite, carbon fiber darks, anodized slate, calibrated racing red accents.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns (Strict Neon Prohibition):
- **Tacky, Absurd & Cyberpunk Neon:** Electric magenta/fuchsia, fluorescent/acid lime green (`#00ff00`, `#91fe01`), blinding cyan (`#00ffff`), or any color tuned to radioactive RGB extremes.
- **Neon Halos & Laser Glows:** CSS neon drop shadows (e.g., `drop-shadow-[0_0_15px_#00ff00]`, `box-shadow: 0 0 25px rgba(...)`), glowing borders, or laser scan lines.
- **Pulsing Neon Lights:** Pulsing status dots (`animate-pulse` on colored dots), flashing lights, or decorative strobe effects.
- **Unnatural Rainbow / RGB Gradients:** Multi-stop neon gradients that shift without architectural context.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Optical Material & Mineral Tones:** All colors must feel grounded in real physical materials (stone, metal, glass, fabric, ink, paper, wood).
- **Matte & Restrained Accents:** Accent colors are applied with restraint (e.g., button fills, subtle hover borders, or typography emphasis) and remain matte, deep, and glare-free.
- **Calibrated Micro-Interactions:** Animations must trigger in response to user intent (hover, scroll, focus, click) using damped spring physics (`stiffness: 280, damping: 24`).

## 4. Zero Generic Templates, AI Clichés, or Dry Wireframes

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- The standard "AI SaaS Dark Mode": Pitch-black `#000` background paired with an oversized radial purple/cyan blur in the center and three identical rounded cards below.
- **Dry Wireframes:** Substituting real imagery or video with abstract geometric SVG boxes or concentric outline circles to avoid rendering real visuals.
- **Complex AI-Fluff Copy:** Convoluted pseudo-scientific vocabulary invented by LLMs to sound technical (e.g., "Zero synthetic escapements", "Algorithmic resonance manifolds", "Kinematic permanence"). Copy must sound human, confident, and grounded.
- Arbitrary rounded corners (`rounded-3xl` or `rounded-[32px]` applied without structural context).
- Generic bento grids filled with random stock icons and fluff metric counters.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Rich Visual Assets & Video Backdrops:** Real luxury photography, macro product close-ups, and 4K video textures (e.g., `https://www.vividsites.app/media/abyss.mp4` for horology/depth themes) integrated with silky backdrop blur overlays.
- **Active Kinematic & Scroll-Scrub Motion:** Use the verified motion mechanics from `shaz-agency-web-design` (e.g., 60fps lerp scroll-scrubbed video, GSAP continuous watch-hand kinematics, spring-damped interactive dials).
- **Swiss Utilitarian Double-Bezel:** Outer tray (`.bezel-tray`) housing an inner card core (`.bezel-core`), establishing architectural hardware depth.
- **Expressive Typography Pairs:** High-contrast serif headlines (`Instrument Serif`, italicized emphasis) contrasted against geometric grotesk or precision monospaced numerals (`JetBrains Mono`).

## 5. Zero Third-Party Branding & Originality Mandate

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- Referencing external design scrapers, competitor platforms, third-party component catalogs, or clone galleries.
- Copying proprietary layout names or promotional text from third-party sites.

### Authorized Patterns:
- All prompt specifications, component architectures, and design tokens must stand as 100% original, proprietary Shaz Agency engineering assets.

## 6. Zero Emojis & Mandatory Monochrome Vector Icons

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Emojis Anywhere in UI:** Using emojis (e.g., 🚀, ✨, 🎉, ⌚, 💎, 🔥) in headlines, buttons, cards, navigation, or features. Emojis immediately destroy editorial elegance and look like cheap amateur templates.
- **Colorful or Multi-Colored Stock Icons:** Clashing icon styles, 3D emoji packs, or multicolored flat icons.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Monochrome Vector Icons:** Use Lucide React or inline SVG exclusively.
- **Calibrated Geometry:** Consistent bounding box (16x16, 20x20, or 24x24), 1.5px to 1.75px uniform stroke width, and `stroke-current` matching typographic hierarchy.

## 7. Zero Comment Leaks, Zero Text-Polluted Video & Zero Irrelevant Imagery

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Syntax & Comment Leaks:** Never leave JSX comment syntax (`{/* ... */}`) in raw HTML files where it renders as visible text on the page.
- **Text-Polluted Background Media:** Never use background videos or images that contain burned-in text, logos, or captions (e.g., videos with embedded titles). Backgrounds must remain purely atmospheric, or use real-time Three.js WebGL / Canvas.
- **Irrelevant Stock Imagery:** Never insert random stock photos that do not match the subject (e.g., cosmetics or coffee on a luxury watch site). Every visual must be rigorously verified, high-resolution, and authentic to the project.
- **Simplistic Wireframe Demonstrations:** Never substitute real products with abstract concentric circles or empty vector wireframe boxes. Deliver full editorial fidelity.

---

## 8. Selective Surface Hierarchy (Never Force Universal Double-Bezel)

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Dogmatic Double-Bezel Caging:** Wrapping every single card, box, table, and media element across an entire page in double-bezel trays (`.bezel-tray` -> `.bezel-core`). This creates monotonous visual fatigue, heavy repetition, and destroys real hierarchy.
- **Double-Bezel in Hero Sections:** Placing double-bezel trays, nested cards, or heavy bordered containers around hero centerpieces, HUDs, execution engines, or interactive models. Hero elements must never be double-layered!

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Deep Lower-Page Accent Only:** Double-Bezel is an occasional, high-status treatment reserved solely for deep lower-page containers (such as flagship tier pricing matrices or settings sheets). It is NEVER permitted in hero sections.
- **Surface Plurality:** Secondary cards, telemetry dossiers, pillar cards, and media modules must utilize clean single hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), flat obsidian panels, borderless floating layouts with divider rules, or subtle surface tonal shifts (`bg-white/[0.02]`).

---

## 9. Zero Post-Hero Telemetry Ticker Strips

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Cockpit Stat Strips Glued to Hero:** Placing a horizontal monospace stat bar / ticker band (e.g., `DAILY RATE: ±0.8S / 24H · 72-HOUR POWER RESERVE · 32 RUBIES · ALLOCATION: 24 PIECES`) directly underneath the hero banner. This mimics generic AI dashboard clutter and breaks cinematic breathing room.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Autonomous Editorial Hero:** The hero section concludes cleanly with spacious negative space.
- **Architectural Placement of Telemetry:** Detailed specifications, frequencies, and tolerances belong inside dedicated technical sections (e.g., a dedicated Calibre or Architecture dossier) or an interactive spec drawer, never as a cluttered ticker strip glued under the hero.

---

## 10. Seamless Atmospheric Section Transitions (Zero Razor Cuts)

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Harsh Full-Bleed Razor Cuts:** Slicing consecutive sections with heavy 1px full-width borders (`border-b border-white/10`) that make the webpage look like severed stacked boxes.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Atmospheric Continuity:** Sections must flow together through deep background color gradients (`bg-gradient-to-b from-[#070709] via-[#090A0E] to-[#070709]`), subtle radial illumination, and soft ambient vignettes.
- **Spacious Negative Space:** Provide generous vertical padding (120px–180px) between major thematic transitions.
- **Container-Confined Dividers:** When separation is necessary, use subtle container-width dividers with gentle edge fades rather than harsh full-bleed horizontal lines.

---

## 11. Hero Elements Purity (Strictly Backgroundless & Never Double-Layered)

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Double-Layered & Boxed Hero Elements:** Wrapping hero centerpieces, execution engines, HUDs, telemetry displays, or 3D models inside double-layered card trays (`.bezel-tray` -> `.bezel-core`), double-bezel borders, or dark boxed backgrounds. Hero elements must NEVER be double-layered!
- **Boxed Hero Specimens:** Enclosing the primary hero centerpiece inside a dark square card frame with corner telemetry labels and borders.
- **Chaotic Mouse-Tilt & Reactive Cursor Tracking:** Adding 3D cursor tilt scripts (`perspective rotateX/rotateY` following mouse coordinates) or camera wobbling that treats a luxury timepiece or engineering specimen like an interactive arcade game.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Strictly Backgroundless Floating Elements:** All hero elements, HUDs, execution telemetry, and visual specimens must float completely backgroundless (`bg-transparent`) directly on the canvas or ambient atmospheric gradient with delicate hairline dividers, zero double-layer enclosures, and zero dark background boxes.
- **Calm, Autonomous Kinematics:** The specimen operates with dignified, serene mechanical precision (e.g., continuous sweeping watch hands via `requestAnimationFrame` and smooth 60s tourbillon cage rotation). Hovering with the mouse does not tilt, wobble, or perturb the object.

## 12. Bespoke Non-Classic Luxury Footers

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Generic 4-Column Corporate Sitemaps:** Slicing the footer into standard boring columns of "Company / About / Careers / Legal / Privacy".
- **Social Media Vanity Grids & Clutter:** Placing colorful social buttons or generic newsletter signup pills at the bottom of a high-end luxury site.

### Authorized Patterns:

- **Live Atelier Timekeeper & Bench Status:** Displaying the real-time local clock of the manufacture (e.g., Geneva CET with live ticking seconds) and master craftsman workbench status.
- **Physical Salons & Private Viewing Concierge:** Physical salon addresses (Geneva, Zurich, London, Tokyo) presented as architectural destinations with direct appointment triggers.
- **Cryptographic Archive Verification:** An interactive serial number check allowing collectors to query observatory testing stability certificates and master watchmaker signature archives.

## 13. Absolute Ban on Eyebrows, Overlines & Micro-Telemetry Clutter

### Terminology & Nature:
- **Eyebrow (Kaş Metni) / Overline / Kicker:** The small, decorative labels placed directly above headlines or section dividers (e.g. `HAUTE HORLOGERIE MANUFACTURE`, `PHYSICAL SALONS & BENCHES`, `TESTING PROTOCOL`, `SECTION 03`).
- **Telemetry / Metadata Clutter (Mikro Bilgi Kirliliği):** Small micro-labels, status snippets, and dimension tags scattered along borders, corners, or divider lines (e.g. `Movement Architecture · 39.5mm · Monocoque`, `FLYING TOURBILLON · SWISS CHRONOMETER`, `6 MASTER WATCHMAKERS ACTIVE`).
- **Why It Is Banned:** AI tools and generic templates habitually clutter every empty space with eyebrows and corner micro-copy to look "technical." In true luxury, architectural, and editorial design, this creates visual noise, looks like an amateur tech cockpit, and dilutes typographic authority.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- Placing any uppercase eyebrow, overline, kicker, or category tag above main section headings.
- Placing header bar labels, subtitle strips, or status strings at the top of a hero section or footer.
- Scattering corner coordinates, dimension tags, or technical sub-labels along card edges and divider lines.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Naked, Authoritative Headlines:** Headings lead directly and commandingly with optical scale, negative space, and typographic contrast. Zero eyebrow prefixes.
- **Clean Content Flow:** Let the headline and purposeful body text carry the narrative without decorative micro-annotations.

## 14. Isolated Transparent Imagery (Zero Boxed Photo Frames)

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Boxed Stock Photos:** Pasting rectangular stock photos with visible room/studio backgrounds inside card frames (e.g. square or 16:9 photo boxes trapped in rectangular card containers).
- **Framed Picture Galleries:** Enclosing luxury products, watch movements, hardware pieces, or architectural specimens inside rigid rectangular photo cards with borders.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Backgroundless / Transparent Cutouts:** All product, hardware, movement, and specimen imagery must be **isolated with transparent backgrounds** (PNG/WebP with transparent alpha or transparent SVG/3D canvas).
- **Direct Atmospheric Integration:** The isolated object floats directly on the page background (e.g. dark obsidian `#070709`), casting soft ambient drop-shadows onto the canvas without any enclosing rectangular photo box or card container.

## 15. Page Architecture Modality (Never Default Dogmatically to One-Page)

### Philosophy & Requirement:
Web designs and digital products are NOT restricted to single-page / one-page formats. Monolithic one-page structures are only suitable for tight single-action landing pages, campaign teasers, or compact atelier showrooms. Complex SaaS platforms, documentation portals, enterprise services, multi-tier e-commerce manufactures, and creative studios require full **Multi-Page Application Architecture**.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- Forcing every user request into an endless single-page scroll with internal anchor hashes (`#overview`, `#features`, `#pricing`) when a multi-page site is requested or needed.
- Jamming comprehensive documentation, privacy legals, multi-product catalogs, and account portals into modal overlays or giant single-page sections.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Scope-Driven Modality Selection:**
  * **One-Page Mode:** Single objective launch pages, campaign teasers, event lineups, or standalone luxury atelier specimen showcases. Navigation uses smooth scroll anchors.
  * **Multi-Page Mode:** Enterprise platforms, multi-product luxury catalogs, documentation hubs, customer portals, and studio case studies.
- **Next.js App Router Multi-Page Standards:**
  * Clean route nesting: `app/(marketing)/about/page.tsx`, `app/(marketing)/features/page.tsx`, `app/(marketing)/pricing/page.tsx`, `app/(marketing)/contact/page.tsx`, `app/(docs)/docs/[...slug]/page.tsx`.
  * Shared persistent layout shells (`layout.tsx`) preserving navbar state and background audio/WebGL contexts across page transitions.
  * Active route indicators using `usePathname()`.
  * Zero jarring page flickers: Sub-pixel opacity cross-fades and exit/enter spring transitions via Framer Motion / template animations.

## 16. Scroll-Morphing Collapsible Dock Navigation

### Philosophy & Requirement:
Navigation must adapt to viewport kinetic intent. When exploring a page, heavy header real estate distracts from editorial focus. The navigation bar must intelligently collapse upon downward scroll into a sleek, floating compact icon dock, and expand seamlessly when scrolling back to the top or when tapped.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Static Sticky Mega-Bars:** Fastening a bulky, full-height header across the viewport during deep reading, obscuring content.
- **Sudden Vanishing Headers:** Harshly hiding the navigation without a visible compact anchor, forcing users to frantically scroll up to find a way out.
- **Jerky CSS Discontinuities:** Toggling navigation modes with abrupt class jumps instead of smooth spring/cubic-bezier geometric transforms.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Kinetic Two-Stage State Machine:**
  * **Top State (`scrollY <= 80px`):** Full-width or container-aligned (`max-w-7xl`) luxury header displaying brand typographic insignia, primary section text links, status indicators, and call-to-action buttons.
  * **Scrolled Dock State (`scrollY > 80px`):** Smoothly contracts (`transition-all duration-300 ease-out`) into a centered, compact floating dock (`width: auto !important; max-width: fit-content !important;`, `rounded-full`, `bg-[#08090C]/94 backdrop-blur-xl border border-white/18 shadow-2xl px-4 py-2`).
- **Deterministic CSS-Enforced Viewport Isolation:**
  * To prevent responsive media queries (e.g. Tailwind `md:flex`, `sm:inline-flex`) from overriding Javascript class changes on desktop viewports, text links (`#fullNavLinks`), brand subtexts, and secondary action buttons must be hidden using parent-scoped CSS rules with `!important` (e.g., `.nav-dock-scrolled #fullNavLinks { display: none !important; }`).
  * The collapsed dock container must dynamically adopt `width: auto !important; max-width: fit-content !important;` so it hugs only the brand insignia and compact icon array without horizontal overflow or text bleeding.
- **Iconic Glyph Morphing:**
  * Full text links gracefully hide, morphing into clean, minimalist monochrome SVG icon buttons (Home, Laws, Blueprints, Architecture, Concierge) with accessible tooltips.
  * An integrated toggle trigger allows users to open a full flyout navigation drawer on demand regardless of scroll position.
  * Scrolling upward or returning to page apex immediately expands the dock back into the full branded bar with zero layout jitter.

## 17. Ecosystem & Technology Integration Infinite Marquee

### Philosophy & Requirement:
For modern technology agencies, enterprise platforms, and digital architectures (exemplified by the `shazagency.com` ecosystem standard), partner and engine integrations must be presented through an ultra-clean, infinite, uninterrupted brand marquee rather than static clunky grid cards.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Static Clunky Logo Grids:** Dumping 10 disparate grayscale sponsor boxes into a heavy rectangular grid table that looks like an old conference sponsor list.
- **Tacky Colored Logos:** Displaying raw corporate brand colors that clash with the site's dark obsidian or calibrated palette.
- **Visible Seam Breaks:** Marquees that stutter, jump, or show empty gaps between loop iterations.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Continuous Sub-Pixel Marquee Belt:**
  * Dual-track infinite CSS marquee (`animation: ecosystemMarquee 35s linear infinite`) moving seamlessly from right to left (`transform: translateX(0)` to `translateX(-50%)`).
  * Seamless pause-on-hover (`:hover { animation-play-state: paused; }`) allowing deliberate inspection.
  * Bilateral edge feathering using modern CSS masks (`mask-image: linear-gradient(to right, transparent 0%, black 8%, black 92%, transparent 100%)`).
- **High-Fidelity Monochromatic SVGs:**
  * Precision vector silhouettes of leading intelligence and infrastructure stacks: Claude, Google, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Shopify, Meta, Stripe, Amazon, Supabase, Apple.
  * Default state: Subtle monochrome slate tone (`#64748b` or `text-slate-400`, `opacity: 0.72`).
  * Hover state: Soft individual brand illumination (e.g. Claude `#D97757`, Supabase `#3ECF8E`, Shopify `#96BF48`, Stripe `#635BFF`) with gentle pill highlight backdrop (`background: rgba(..., 0.08)`).
- **Hairline Integration Framing:**
  * Encapsulated inside a single hairline card or borderless ambient band with a refined uppercase tracking kicker, free of decorative pills or generic badges.

## 18. Bespoke Vector Favicon Architecture (Every Project Demands Tailored Brand Identity)

### Philosophy & Requirement:
Every website, digital product, or client application must possess an original, vector-based SVG favicon tailored directly to its brand identity, material palette, and sector motif. Favicons are the primary persistent visual anchor in browser tabs, bookmarks, and mobile home screens; generic browser defaults, missing icons, emojis, or uncalibrated raster images destroy high-end brand perception.

### Forbidden Anti-Patterns:
- **Missing or Default Framework Favicons:** Leaving default browser or framework icons (such as Vercel triangle, Next.js 'N', or generic globe).
- **Emojis as Favicons:** Using raw unicode emojis (e.g. `<link rel="icon" href="data:image/svg+xml,<text>...</text>">`). Emojis appear childish, render inconsistently across operating systems, and violate Law 6.
- **Low-Resolution Raster Favicons:** Blurry 16x16 or 32x32 `.ico` or `.png` files that look pixelated on Retina and 4K displays.
- **Interchangeable / Copied Favicons:** Reusing identical icons across different client domains without tailoring to the specific industry.

### Authorized Patterns:
- **Bespoke Scalable Vector SVG:** Clean, scalable SVG vectors built with mathematical precision, consistent bounding geometry, and theme-aligned gradient stops.
- **Industry & Material Alignment:**
  * *Agency / Technology / Systems (e.g., Shaz Agency):* Dark obsidian squircle base with hairline border, high-contrast titanium geometric monogram, and vibrant electric cyan accent node.
  * *Haute Horlogerie / Luxury Watches (e.g., Kronos):* Circular brushed titanium / gold bezel with fine index graduations, dial guilloché depth, and minimalist watch hands.
  * *Architecture / Industrial Studio:* Monolithic geometric monolith, concrete gray tone with chamfered bevel corners and structural balance.
  * *Biotech / Medical / Quant:* Precision helical nodes or calibrated stepped coordinate grid marks.
- **Dual-Delivery Architecture:**
  * Standalone asset file placed at root: `/favicon.svg`.
  * Explicit HTML head declaration: `<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="favicon.svg">`.
  * Optional inline SVG Data URI fallback for self-contained single-file demos.
