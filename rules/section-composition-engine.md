# Shaz Agency Web Design — Section Composition Engine

This guide dictates how sections are sequenced, structured, and visually balanced based on real user goals and sector archetypes, rather than generic landing page formulas.

---

## 1. Composition Archetypes by Sector

### Archetype 1: Editorial Magazine & Cultural Journal
* **Best For:** Independent fashion, architectural reviews, literary journals, cultural publications, design retrospectives.
* **Base Palette:** Warm Swiss Paper (`#F4F1EA`), Deep Ink (`#111113`), Smoked Umber (`#4A4238`), Burnt Ochre accents.
* **Typography:** `Instrument Serif` (Editorial Italics) + `Neue Haas Grotesk` (Subheads) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).
* **Section Order (Non-Standard Flow):**
  1. *Masthead Banner & Vol/Issue Ledger* (Top bar with date, issue number, current editorial topic).
  2. *Monolithic Typographic Title Hero* (Zero 3D, zero right-column card; pure high-scale typography spanning 90% width).
  3. *Featured Monograph Curtain* (Full-width high-resolution photography with sticky editorial commentary).
  4. *Asymmetric Dual-Column Essay Grid* (Left 35% author perspective; right 65% primary narrative thesis).
  5. *Curated Visual Gallery with Horizontal Pull* (Scroll-scrubbed gallery showing tactile prints).
  6. *Editorial Index & Colophon Footer* (Contributors list, typographic watermark, print circulation numbers).

### Archetype 2: Experimental & Kinetic Music Festival / Avant-Garde
* **Best For:** Underground electronic festivals, generative art showcases, experimental sound labs, subculture drops.
* **Base Palette:** Pitch Black (`#0A0A09`), High-Voltage Acid Lime (`#91FE01`) or Toxic Magenta (`#FF007F`), Molten Chrome, Stark White.
* **Typography:** `Tilt Warp` or `Druk Wide 700` (Deconstructed Headlines) + `JetBrains Mono` (Set Times & Frequencies).
* **Section Order:**
  1. *Deconstructed Lineup Masthead* (High-speed marquee ticker + brutalist full-bleed date lockup).
  2. *Kinetic Distortion Hero* (WebGL reactive audio mesh `library/05-3d-webgl/vortex.md` or `plasma-burst.md` + massive title).
  3. *Soundstage Matrix & Time Schedule* (Interactive stage switcher: Stage A, Stage B, Ambient Dome with live timestamps).
  4. *Audiovisual Specimen Reel* (Horizontal drag-inertia track of performer dossiers).
  5. *Tiered Pass Allocation Ledger* (High-contrast ticket tiers with countdown timer to door opening).
  6. *Brutalist Direct Action Footer* (Massive footer headline spanning edge-to-edge, zero copyright fluff, direct pass wallet).

### Archetype 3: Minimalist Architecture & Spatial Monograph
* **Best For:** High-end architecture practices, industrial design firms, interior monolithic studios.
* **Base Palette:** Raw Concrete (`#E8E6E1`), Basalt Slate (`#18181B`), Chalk (`#FAF9F6`), Muted Graphite (`#52525B`).
* **Typography:** `Neue Haas Grotesk` (Light & Medium) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` + Monospaced Dimensions.
* **Section Order:**
  1. *Minimalist Double-Bezel Header* (Discrete top island hugging the apex).
  2. *Full-Bleed Monograph Hero* (Single architectural photo with ambient fade; title placed at lower-left border).
  3. *Spatial Philosophy Statement* (Huge serif or grotesque statement with 60% negative space around it).
  4. *Horizontal Case-Study Cascade* (Sticky scroll rail tracking built projects with square meters, latitude, material billets).
  5. *Materiality & Tectonic Dossier* (Tactile material samples: honed basalt, cast bronze, hand-troweled lime).
  6. *Editorial Studio Footer* (Direct studio coordinates: Zurich, Basel, Tokyo; direct principal partner contact).

### Archetype 4: FinTech & Data-Dense Intelligence Suite
* **Best For:** Quant trading platforms, institutional asset managers, risk telemetry dashboards, API gateways.
* **Base Palette:** Deep Basalt (`#090A0E`), Ice Slate (`#F8FAFC`), Platinum Hairline (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), Jade (`#10B981`) or Cobalt (`#3B82F6`) telemetry markers.
* **Typography:** `JetBrains Mono` (Primary Data) + `Spline Sans` (Interface) + `Druk Wide` (Hero Title).
* **Section Order:**
  1. *Telemetry Command Bar* (System operational health, P99 latency, global market status).
  2. *Monolithic Data Hero* (Live stepped SVG area chart `library/11-dashboards-data-viz/financial-analytics-monolith.md` + direct execution CTA).
  3. *Live Order / Protocol Stream* (Real-time sub-pixel counter reveals showing throughput metrics).
  4. *Contrast Surface Architecture* (Inverted dark core capability matrix contrasting against light stone background).
  5. *Cryptographic Compliance & SOC2 Proof Ledger* (Audited vault certificates and API key generator).
  6. *Compact Utility Ledger Footer* (No vanity social icons; raw server status, API docs link, encrypted support key).

### Archetype 5: Organic Earth & Sustainable Agriculture
* **Best For:** Biodynamic wine estates, regenerative farming cooperatives, sustainable heirloom foods, craft roasteries.
* **Base Palette:** Unbleached Natural Kraft (`#F5F2EB`), Forest Olive (`#2C362B`), Terracotta (`#A45A3D`), Warm Clay (`#D9CEBE`).
* **Typography:** Classic Warm Serif (`Instrument Serif` or `Fraunces`) + Clean Humanist Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
* **Zero 3D Mandate:** Completely eliminate WebGL 3D models. Substitute with high-resolution macro soil/leaf photography or subtle procedural CSS terrain noise.
* **Section Order:**
  1. *Provenance Top Bar* (Current harvest season, terroir coordinates, biodynamic certification hallmark).
  2. *Split-Screen Terroir Hero* (Left: Quiet authoritative headline on harvest philosophy; Right: Isolated transparent bottle or crop cutout with natural shadow).
  3. *Soil & Climate Telemetry* (Micro-climate data: annual rainfall, elevation, sun exposure hours in clean hairline tables).
  4. *Seasonal Release Ledger* (Limited batch allocations, bottle edition numbers, direct cellar door reservation).
  5. *Grower's Journal / Almanac* (Chronological seasonal diary with dates, pruning milestones, fermentation notes).
  6. *Provenance Seal Footer* (Farm map, cellar visiting hours, direct agricultural cooperative contact).

### Archetype 6: Cosmic Exploration & Interactive Science Lab
* **Best For:** Deep-space observatories, astrophysics research, planetary geology portals, particle physics simulators.
* **Base Palette:** Deep Cosmic Void (`#040407`), Stellar Nebula Violet (`#1E1035`), Comet Cyan (`#38BDF8`), Solar Amber (`#F59E0B`).
* **Typography:** `Space Grotesk` (Hero) + `JetBrains Mono` (Telemetry & Coordinates).
* **Section Order:**
  1. *Observatory Status Nav* (Telescope focal aperture, sky coordinates, UTC epoch clock).
  2. *Volumetric Particle Canvas Hero* (`library/05-3d-webgl/cosmic-web.md` or `wave-galaxy.md` with interactive gravity drag).
  3. *Orbital Parameters Dossier* (Scientific telemetry: orbital velocity, aphelion, perihelion, mass metrics).
  4. *Interactive Spectral Analyzer* (Clickable frequency sliders shifting shader wavelengths from infrared to ultraviolet).
  5. *Deep Space Archive Grid* (Monochrome vector constellation maps and telescope capture logs).
  6. *Observatory Network Footer* (Ground station links: Atacama, Mauna Kea, La Palma; open-source data telemetry feed).

## 2. Dynamic Section Sequencing Protocol

When structuring any website:
1. Identify the **Primary Proof Point** of the user. If the proof point is a live working chart, put it immediately in the Hero. If the proof point is a performer lineup, put it in Section 2.
2. Never repeat the identical card style in two consecutive sections. If Section 2 uses single hairline border cards, Section 3 must use an open text ledger or full-bleed horizontal divider layout.
3. Vary vertical heights: Alternate between tall atmospheric sections (`min-h-[80vh]`) and compact high-density data matrices (`py-16`).
