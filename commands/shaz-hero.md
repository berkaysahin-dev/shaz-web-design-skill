# /shaz-hero — High-Cinematic Diverse Hero Section Architect

Use this skill when the user runs `/shaz-hero [brief or industry]` or requests a premier hero section adhering to Shaz Agency standards.

---

## 1. Absolute Anti-Pattern Bans (Laws 2, 3, 6, 9, 11, 20)

1. **NO 50/50 SPLIT CLICHÉ (Law 20):** Never place a headline and text on the left (50%) and a boxed 3D model/card on the right (50%). This is an amateur AI template habit.
2. **NO GRAPHIC LOGOS (Law 6, Law 21):** Zero graphic logos, SVG emblems, shield icons, or app icon boxes. Only naked typographic wordmarks or raw coordinates.
3. **NO BLINKING / PULSING LIGHTS (Law 3, Law 20):** Zero `animate-pulse`, blinking dots, radar pings, or flashing telemetry tags in any stage.
4. **NO TOY 3D MESHES (Law 22):** Zero naive 10-line primitive shapes (cylinders with confetti cubes). Only production-grade PBR shaders from `library/05-3d-webgl/` or Zero-WebGL with macro photography.
5. **NO POST-HERO TICKER STRIPS (Law 9):** Never glue a horizontal stat bar directly under the hero banner.

---

## 2. The 6 Mandatory Hero Archetypes

When generating a hero section, explicitly declare and execute one of these 6 archetypes:

### Archetype A: Full-Bleed Ambient Canvas / Shader Hero
* **Layout Structure:** A real-time Three.js canvas or GLSL fragment shader spans the entire background (`fixed` or `absolute inset-0 w-full h-full pointer-events-none`).
* **Content Placement:** Authoritative headline sits centered or lower-third directly over the ambient space. Zero separate right-side specimen box.
* **Best For:** Haute Horlogerie, deep-tech platforms, luxury perfumes, spatial computing.

### Archetype B: Asymmetric Monumental Typography Hero
* **Layout Structure:** High-scale display typography (`Instrument Serif`, `Druk Wide`) spans 80–90% of the viewport width at the apex.
* **Content Placement:** Underneath the headline, an asymmetric offset ledger holds 1 dense factual sentence and an interactive focal trigger (e.g. spring drawer or glyph scrubber).
* **Best For:** Architecture practices, design monographs, industrial studios.

### Archetype C: Kinetic Horizontal Panoramic Strip Hero
* **Layout Structure:** Full-viewport horizontal panoramic viewport.
* **Content Placement:** Static left vertical mast anchor with edge-to-edge interactive drag-inertia coverflow rail (`library/10-interactive-animations/templates/monolith.md`).
* **Best For:** Heavy industrial equipment, automotive platforms, luxury real estate, fashion catalogs.

### Archetype D: Fullscreen Monolithic Editorial Curtain
* **Layout Structure:** Full-screen high-resolution architectural or material monograph curtain that reveals an editorial index upon initial scroll.
* **Content Placement:** Quiet typography anchored to the bottom-left corner with hairline coordinates.
* **Best For:** Editorial journals, fine art foundations, architectural retrospectives.

### Archetype E: Split Typography Hero (Editorial Dialogue)
* **Layout Structure:** Two columns of pure typography: Left column contains the principal thesis, right column contains the counter-thesis or architectural colophon.
* **Content Placement:** The specimen is an ambient central particle field or absent entirely.
* **Best For:** Research labs, economic forums, legal platforms, philosophy journals.

### Archetype F: Zero-Specimen Typographic Stage Hero
* **Layout Structure:** 100% typographic and motion-driven stage.
* **Content Placement:** Uses interactive character-scrubbing letterforms (`library/10-interactive-animations/components/glyph.md`) or kinetic spring matrices. Zero 3D meshes.
* **Best For:** Modern developer tools, typography ateliers, Swiss utilitarian interfaces.

## 3. Explanatory Output Protocol

When executing `/shaz-hero`:
1. **Selected Hero Archetype:** Declare which of the 6 archetypes is selected and explain the spatial hierarchy.
2. **Applied Universal Design Laws:** Cite the governing laws (Laws 1, 2, 3, 6, 8, 9, 11, 20).
3. **Motion & Canvas Breakdown:** Detail the shaders, camera vectors, spring physics, or video textures.
4. **Production Next.js / Tailwind Code:** Output complete, copy-pasteable TypeScript code with zero placeholders.
