## 1. Absolute Anti-Pattern Bans (Laws 1, 2, 3, 11, 12, 13, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23)

1. **NO 50/50 SPLIT CLICHÉ (Law 20):** Never place a headline and paragraph on the left 50% and a 3D model/card box on the right 50%.
2. **NO UPPER / CORNER MICRO-TEXTS (Law 13):** Strictly zero corner micro-labels (`BENCH 04`, `STAGE 02`, `NODE 01`), zero floating canvas telemetry lines (`• REAL-TIME...`), and zero card-header subtitle strips (`SERIES 01 · BASALT`). Visual surfaces must breathe with pure, uncluttered clarity.
3. **NO GRAPHIC LOGOS (Law 19):** Strictly zero graphic logo emblems, shield badges, circular crests, or app icon boxes next to the brand name in the navigation header. Use pure, naked typographic wordmarks only.
4. **NO FORMULA SLIDERS OR 3-KPI BOXES (Law 21):** Strictly zero copy-pasting of the "3 KPI tiles + 2D canvas curve + range slider" formula. Deploy the specialized mechanics defined in this skill.
5. **NO TOY 3D MESHES (Law 22):** Strictly zero naive 10-line primitive shapes (cylinders with confetti cubes, basic unshaded wireframe boxes). Use verified production-grade PBR shaders or enforce the Zero-WebGL mandate with tactile macro photography and SVG grain.
6. **NO BLINKING / PULSING LIGHTS (Law 3, Law 23):** Strictly zero `animate-pulse`, blinking dots, or radar pings anywhere in UI, 3D stages, or telemetry boxes. Calm, matte, static markers only.
7. **NO FLUFF TEXT & NO EYEBROWS (Laws 1 & 2):** Headlines lead directly with optical scale; body copy is strictly limited to 1 dense factual sentence maximum per section (or 0 sentences). Zero uppercase eyebrow tags above headings.
8. **NO CORPORATE FOOTER SITEMAPS (Law 12):** Deploy the **Architectural Master Horizon Footer**: an authoritative dialogue invitation sentence, clean symmetrical navigation deck without micro-labels, physical destinations in pure typography, and a balanced horizon baseline with discreet local time.
