# Shaz Design System — Layout Diversity & Creative Direction

True design diversity is structural, compositional, and typographic — not simply swapping an accent hex code or background tone.

---

## 1. Creative Direction Mandate

Operate with senior creative direction across every project:
1. **Analyze Brand Character & DNA:** Decode market positioning (luxury, corporate, technical, radical, accessible, clinical).
2. **Evaluate Sector Conventions:** Determine where to align with user expectations and where to decisively break away to achieve market dominance.
3. **Formulate Emotional Anchor:** Identify the precise physical feeling the design must evoke (e.g. calm mastery, architectural weight, speed, tactile warmth, clinical precision).
4. **Enforce Optical Hierarchy:** Scale headings to command attention nakedly; banish all decorative kicker micro-tags.
5. **Layout-First Composition:** Derive page structure from user journey and content requirements before selecting components.

---

## 2. Creative Production Intelligence

### A. Intelligent Project Discovery
Before writing code or selecting blueprints, analyze the real business context:
- **Business Model & Sub-Niche:** Distinguish between developer platforms, enterprise consultancies, luxury ateliers, boutique clinics, and bespoke commerce.
- **Conversion Objective:** Immediate technical dialogue, frictionless checkout, portfolio inspection, or strategic qualification.
- **Audience Expectation:** Senior decision-makers demand factual precision, clean typography, and zero fluff; consumer lifestyle demands atmospheric emotion and tactile pacing.

### B. Visual Asset Strategy
Images and interface showcases must never be decorative filler:
- **Software:** Authentic interface mockups, live terminal output, cluster metrics, and interactive composers.
- **Architecture & Industrial:** Full-bleed architectural photography, structural materials (travertine, steel, glass), and engineering blueprints.
- **Gastronomy & Hospitality:** Sensory food close-ups, spatial dining atmosphere, and curated menus.
- **Automotive & Motorsports:** Dynamic low-angle chassis photography, telemetry streams, and aerodynamic surfaces.
- **Zero Generic Stock Photos:** Never use smiling corporate stock models, generic lightbulbs, or abstract AI floating cubes.

### C. Advanced Interaction Design
Motion must serve cognitive clarity, not visual ornamentation:
- Use physical spring transitions, contextual micro-interactions, progressive disclosures, and tabbed architectural views.
- Strictly ban spinning badges, glowing laser borders, strobe blinks, and unnecessary floating objects.

---

## 3. Creative Exploration Mode (3 Strategic Directions)

When exploring options or establishing art direction, formulate 3 distinct avenues:

- **Direction A — Safe & Premium (Piyasa Standardının Zirvesi):**
  Flawless execution of proven high-end patterns. Impeccable typography, familiar ergonomics, maximum user trust and conversion clarity.
- **Direction B — Bold & Distinct (Karakterli ve Ayrışan):**
  Unexpected color polarity (e.g. pristine white for tech, deep oxblood for law), asymmetrical grid structures, high-contrast editorial typography.
- **Direction C — Experimental (Sınırları Zorlayan):**
  Progressive interaction mechanics, horizontal scrubbers, architectural ledgers, generative SVG or WebGL backgrounds.

---

## 4. Structural Layout Diversity & Anti-Template Matrix

Never default to the same layout formula:
- **Hero Archetypes:** Full-Bleed Ambient Canvas · Asymmetric Monumental Typography · Kinetic Horizontal Strip · Fullscreen Editorial Curtain · Split Typography · Zero-Specimen Typographic Stage.
- **Navigation Archetypes:** Floating Minimalist Capsule Dock · Full-Bleed Editorial Horizon · Asymmetric Split Drawer · Dual-Tier Precision Utility Header.
- **Services & Features:** Editorial Numbered Index · Image-Led Split Composition · Asymmetric Modular Grid · Interactive Friction Drawer · Horizon Ledger.
- **Footers:** Architectural Master Horizon with direct dialogue inquiry.

---

## 5. Cross-Project Decision Memory

To prevent consecutive outputs in a workspace or conversation from converging into a single personal style:
- The system tracks: Background tone (light/dark) · Primary font pairing · Accent tone · Hero archetype · Navigation model · Container scale.
- If consecutive projects share identical traits, the system actively diverts to an unrepresented polarity.
