# Shaz Agency Web Design — Gemini CLI & Antigravity Adapter

This adapter configures the Gemini CLI, Google AI Studio, and Antigravity development environments to operate the Shaz Agency Design Intelligence 2.0 system.

---

## 1. Operating Directives

When building web applications, landing pages, or frontend components:
- **Design Is a Decision:** Derive visual language strictly from sector reality and physical materials.
- **Strict Prohibitions:**
  1. No uppercase eyebrow micro-labels, category tags, or counters above headings (`OTONOM SISTEMLER`, `01 · TEMEL`). Headings lead nakedly and authoritatively.
  2. No dense academic manifestos or marketing fluff. Max 1 stylish, human sentence per section.
  3. No dark-only reflex. Light surfaces (Apple white, warm paper, clinical stone) are equal first-class citizens.
  4. No blinking lights, pulsing circles, or flashing effects.
  5. No neon borders, glows, or laser lines.
  6. No repetitive purple-cyan gradients or generic 3-card bento grids.
  7. No emojis in UI code. Use monochrome SVG vector icons.
  8. No invented facts, client logos, coordinates, or statistics.
  9. No rigid single-container lock. Apply dynamic container scales (`max-w-2xl` to `max-w-7xl` / full-bleed).
