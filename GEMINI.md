# Shaz Agency Web Design — Gemini CLI & Antigravity Adapter

This adapter configures the Gemini CLI, Google AI Studio, and Antigravity development environments to operate the Shaz Agency Design Intelligence 2.0 system with motion choreography, color consistency, and content integrity.

---

## 1. Operating Directives

When building web applications, landing pages, or frontend components:
- **Design Is a Decision:** Derive visual language strictly from sector reality, purposeful motion, coherent color tokens, and authentic content.
- **Strict Prohibitions:**
  1. No uppercase eyebrow micro-labels, category tags, or counters above headings (`OTONOM SISTEMLER`, `01 · TEMEL`). Headings lead nakedly and authoritatively.
  2. No meaningless filler microcopy or fake status badges (`Küme sağlıklı`, `Sistem aktif`, `Yenilikçi yaklaşım`, `Geleceği şekillendiriyoruz`). Apply the Content Necessity Test.
  3. No dense academic manifestos or marketing fluff. Max 1 stylish, human sentence per section.
  4. No disconnected ad-hoc hex colors. Define centralized semantic color tokens upfront.
  5. No lifeless static elements or non-working controls. Implement tactile hover lifts and working tabs/forms.
  6. No dark-only reflex. Light surfaces (Apple white, warm paper, clinical stone) are equal first-class citizens.
  7. No blinking lights, pulsing circles, or flashing effects.
  8. No neon borders, glows, or laser lines.
  9. No repetitive purple-cyan gradients or generic 3-card bento grids.
  10. No emojis in UI code. Use monochrome SVG vector icons.
  11. No invented facts, client logos, coordinates, or statistics.
  12. No rigid single-container lock. Apply dynamic container scales (`max-w-2xl` to `max-w-7xl` / full-bleed).
